# KB01 · Billing cycle and due dates
Acme Fibre bills are generated on the 1st of every month and are due by the 10th. Customers can see every bill in the Acme app under Billing. The plan price is charged in advance for the month.

# KB02 · Late payment fee
If a bill is not paid by the 10th, a late fee of ₹100 is added to the next bill. The late fee is charged only once per month. Agents may waive the late fee once a year for customers with more than 12 months of tenure.

# KB03 · Payment methods
Customers can pay by UPI, debit card, credit card or net banking in the Acme app, or set up AutoPay. Acme never asks for card numbers or OTPs over phone, email or chat.

# KB04 · Refunds and credits
Double payments are refunded automatically within 5 to 7 working days. Agents can approve refunds or credits of up to ₹500. Anything above ₹500 needs approval from the billing team: tell the customer it has been sent for approval, and escalate.

# KB05 · Outages and restoration updates
When an area has an outage, share the outage ticket ID and the current status. Never promise an exact time or date for restoration. Engineers send updates by email and in the Acme app. Suggest mobile data in the meantime.

# KB06 · Outage credit
If an outage lasts more than 24 hours, a credit of one day's charge for every day without service is added to the next bill automatically. The customer does not need to ask for it.

# KB07 · Slow speed: first checks
Ask the customer to restart the router (switch it off for 30 seconds), then test the speed on a cable connection at fast.com. For Wi-Fi, move closer to the router. Evening speeds on Wi-Fi can be lower when many devices are connected.

# KB08 · Technician visits
If the speed on a cable connection is below half of the plan speed after a restart, book a technician visit. Visits are free and happen within 2 working days.

# KB09 · Plan upgrades and downgrades
Plans: Basic 100 Mbps for ₹699 per month, Plus 300 Mbps for ₹999 per month, Pro 1 Gbps for ₹1,499 per month. Upgrades are active within 1 hour and the difference is charged pro-rata on the next bill. Downgrades start from the next billing cycle.

# KB10 · Cancellation and notice period
Cancellation needs 30 days of notice. The router is collected from home free of charge. The final bill includes charges up to the end of the notice period.

# KB11 · Retention offer
Customers with more than 12 months of tenure who ask to cancel can be offered 50% off for 1 month. Offer it once, politely, and do not push if they still want to cancel.

# KB12 · Shifting your connection
Shifting the connection is free within our Pune service areas: Kothrud, Baner, Aundh, Hinjewadi, Wakad, Viman Nagar, Kharadi and Hadapsar. Re-installation happens within 48 hours of the requested date. Outside these areas, the connection has to be cancelled with 30 days of notice.

# KB13 · Router and Wi-Fi password
To change the Wi-Fi password, open the Acme app and go to Wi-Fi settings, or open 192.168.1.1 in a browser and sign in with the details on the router's sticker. Never ask the customer to share their password.

# KB14 · New installation
New connections are installed within 48 hours after document checks (address proof and photo ID).

# KB15 · Account security
Acme staff never ask for OTPs, passwords or full card numbers. If someone asked for an OTP and claimed to be from Acme, tell the customer not to share it, and escalate it as a possible fraud attempt.

# KB16 · Talking to a human
Escalate to a human when the customer threatens legal action, threatens to leave after repeated complaints, asks for a refund or credit above ₹500, or reports possible fraud.
