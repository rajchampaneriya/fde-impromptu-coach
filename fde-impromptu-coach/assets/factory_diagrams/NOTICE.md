# Gas City diagrams (rendered)

These PNGs are 2x renders of the Excalidraw diagrams in the Gas City
repository, `docs/diagrams/excalidraw-rendered/*.svg`, taken from
https://github.com/gastownhall/gascity at commit
e244b16f3a764e0654f0784cba648a98e0aed54c (2026-10-02). They are used
unchanged on the Software Factory video slides, with credit on the slide.

To refresh them after the upstream diagrams change, render each SVG to a
white-background PNG at twice its viewBox size (any browser screenshot works)
and keep the same file names.
